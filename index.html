HEALTHY LIFESTYLE

Build habits that last

Health is not one big decision. It is the collection of small choices you make every day.

🥗

Eat balanced

Choose a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, protein and healthy fats.

🏃

Stay active

Move regularly throughout the day and choose activities you genuinely enjoy.

😴

Sleep well

Keep a consistent sleep routine and give your body enough time to recover.

🧠

Care for your mind

Take breaks, manage stress and make time for hobbies, friends and relaxation.

HEALTHY FOOD

SLEEP & WATER TIPS

Your daily reset

💧

Hydration

  • Keep water nearby during the day.
  • Drink regularly rather than waiting until very thirsty.
  • Increase fluids when you sweat more or the weather is hot.
🌙

Better sleep

  • Keep your sleep and wake times consistent.
  • Wind down and reduce bright screens before bed.
  • Keep your bedroom comfortable, quiet and dark.
